About Smart Farmers Hub Uganda

Smart Farmers Hub connects farmers directly with buyers while offering AI advice, weather forecasting, online payments, and dependable delivery services.

Business Name: Smart Farmers Hub Uganda

Official Slogan: Empowering Farmers. Feeding the Future.

Our Story

Many farmers in Mbarara struggle to find reliable buyers, receive fair prices, transportation, and credible agricultural information. Smart Farmers Hub was created to solve this challenge with technology and trust.

Vision

To become Uganda's leading digital agriculture platform by empowering rural producers and strengthening the food value chain.

Mission

To empower farmers using technology that increases incomes, reduces losses, and exposes them to wider markets.

Objectives

  • Connect more than 500 farmers
  • Increase farmer income
  • Reduce post-harvest losses
  • Provide AI farming support
  • Expand throughout Western Uganda
